Notch privacy

Notch is made by ZeroOrigine. This page says, in plain words, what Notch keeps and what it sends.

What stays on your device

Your chat history, the things you ask Nova to remember, your timer and your settings. They are stored in your browser or app, not on our servers. "New chat" clears the history.

What is sent to Nova's brain

When you ask Nova something it cannot answer on the device, Notch sends your recent messages, the things you asked it to remember, and a random device number to ZeroOrigine's notch-api. The Chrome version also sends the title and address of the page you are on. notch-api passes the messages to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer.

What our server keeps

For each answer: the time, a scrambled (hashed) device number, a scrambled network address, the app type, the size of the answer and its cost. We keep this to stop abuse and to stay within our daily budget. We do not keep the text of your messages.

Accounts

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with your email, we keep your email address in our shared ZeroOrigine account system so you can sign in again.

Microphone

Voice is used only when you tap the mic. Speech is turned into text by your browser or device. In Chrome, the microphone permission belongs to Notch itself, never to the website you are on.

No ads, no selling

Notch has no ads and no trackers. We never sell or share your data.
