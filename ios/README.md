# Notch for iPhone and iPad

1. Install Xcode from the App Store, open it once.
2. Double-click build-ios.command. It makes the project and opens Xcode.
3. In Xcode: click "NotchiOS" (blue icon, top left) > each target (Notch, NovaIsland) > Signing & Capabilities > Team: pick your Apple ID.
4. Plug in your iPhone, pick it at the top of Xcode, press the Play button.
5. On the iPhone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > trust your Apple ID.
